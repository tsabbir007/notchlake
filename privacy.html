NotchLake Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

NotchLake is a Mac app that shows widgets in the notch at the top of your screen. This policy explains what it does with your information. Short version: NotchLake does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and has no analytics or tracking.

Data that stays on your Mac Notes, scratchpad text, clipboard history, shelf items, prompter scripts, timers, screen time, settings and widget layout are stored only on your Mac, inside NotchLake's own app container. Deleting the app deletes them.

Clipboard history skips items that apps mark as concealed or transient (for example, passwords from password managers).

Microphone and speech Voice mode in the Prompter uses Apple's speech recognition on your device only. Audio is processed in memory to follow your reading and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Location and weather If you use the Weather widget, your approximate location is used to request a forecast from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). It is not sent to us. You can type a city instead of sharing your location.

Calendar and reminders Events and reminders are read and edited through macOS on your Mac. They are not sent to us.

Music and Spotify NotchLake asks Music and Spotify on your Mac what's playing so it can show and control it. Spotify artwork is downloaded from Spotify's servers.

Revenue (optional) If you add an API key for Stripe, Polar, Dodo Payments or Lemon Squeezy, it is stored in your macOS Keychain and sent only to that provider to fetch your sales. We never see it.

Links If you save links, NotchLake fetches each site's icon directly from that site.

Children NotchLake is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
